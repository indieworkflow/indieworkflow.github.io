How to Write a CV in 2026 (Step-by-Step Guide)

Published May 2026 · 5 min read

Writing a CV is one of the most important steps in getting hired. In this guide, you will learn exactly how to create a modern CV that gets interviews in 2026.

What employers look for in a CV

Recruiters spend only 6–10 seconds scanning a CV. That means your CV must be clear, simple and easy to scan.

  • Clear structure
  • Relevant skills
  • Results and achievements
  • No unnecessary information

Step 1 — Write a strong headline

Your headline should immediately explain who you are.

Examples:

  • Junior Web Developer
  • Customer Support Specialist
  • Marketing Assistant

Step 2 — Add a short summary

Your summary should be 2–3 sentences explaining your skills and goals.

Step 3 — Focus on achievements

Instead of listing tasks, show results.

Bad: Responsible for social media

Good: Grew Instagram page from 0 to 5k followers in 6 months

Step 4 — Keep it simple

Modern CVs should be clean and easy to read. Avoid graphics and unnecessary design.
