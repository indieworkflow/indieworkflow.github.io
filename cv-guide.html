How to Write a CV That Gets Interviews

5 min read

Writing a CV is one of the most important steps in getting hired. In this guide, you will learn how to create a modern CV that stands out and increases your chances of getting interviews.

What Employers Look for in a CV

Recruiters spend only a few seconds scanning a CV. That means your CV must be clear, simple and easy to read.

  • Clear structure
  • Relevant skills
  • Results and achievements
  • No unnecessary information

Step 1 — Write a Strong Headline

Your headline should immediately explain who you are.

Examples:

  • Junior Web Developer
  • Customer Support Specialist
  • Marketing Assistant

Step 2 — Add a Short Summary

Your summary should be 2–3 sentences explaining your skills, experience and career goals.

Step 3 — Focus on Achievements

Instead of listing tasks, show results.

Weak: Responsible for social media

Better: Grew Instagram page from 0 to 5,000 followers in 6 months

Step 4 — Keep It Simple

Modern CVs should be clean and easy to read. Avoid unnecessary graphics, colors and complicated layouts.

A great CV is not about fancy design. It is about clearly showing employers who you are, what you can do and the value you can bring. Focus on clarity, achievements and relevance, and you will immediately stand out from many other applicants.
